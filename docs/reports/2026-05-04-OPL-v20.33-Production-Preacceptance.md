## Commercial Trial Boundary

v20.33 may enter controlled commercial trial operation only after:

- full loop evidence has `ok=true`;
- all full loop stages have `ok=true`;
- resource deletion evidence includes billing stop or clear stop state;
- official hosts prove application build tag `opl-v20.33`;
- rollback commands, image digests, rollout history, migration job, and backup point are recorded.

Until those live gates pass, v20.33 remains a local implementation and preacceptance candidate, not a commercial production release.
